Irene Casademont Receives the Alumni UdG Award 2026

On July 8, the University of Girona (UdG) presented Dr. Irene Casademont, former member of the Institut de Química Computacional i Catàlisi (IQCC), with the Alumni UdG Award 2026, in recognition of her scientific career and contributions to research and knowledge.

Irene graduated in Chemistry from the University of Girona and completed the Master in Advanced Catalysis and Molecular Modelling at the Institute of Computational Chemistry and Catalysis (IQCC). She is currently a postdoctoral researcher at the Donostia International Physics Center (DIPC), where she continues an international research career focused on computational approaches and advanced materials.

The Alumni UdG Awards recognize the talent, achievements, and societal impact of former students of the University of Girona.

Congratulations, Irene!
