Jesus Receives the Distinction of Bergara’s First Ambassador in Recognition of His Scientific Career

Jesús M. Ugalde Named Bergara’s First Ambassador in Recognition of His Scientific Career

Jesús M. Ugalde Named Bergara’s First Ambassador in Recognition of His Scientific Career

Today, Jesús M. Ugalde Uribe-Etxebarria, Professor of Physical Chemistry at the University of the Basque Country (EHU), received the first Bergara ZTB Ambassador Award.

The distinction was presented during the first Tungsten Day, held to commemorate the isolation of tungsten by the Elhuyar brothers in Bergara in 1783. The award, established by Bergara City Council and the Laboratorium Museum, recognizes Ugalde’s outstanding scientific career and his contributions to research, scientific outreach, and the international visibility of Bergara.

A member of Kimika Teorikoa and former President of Jakiunde (2012–2020), Ugalde has received numerous distinctions throughout his career, including the Euskadi Research Award in 2003.

The ceremony took place today at Seminarixoa in Bergara.
