Coarse-grained simulations of immobilised enzymes

We use coarse-grained molecular simulations with the Martini force field to study enzymes and other biomolecules immobilised on surfaces. A key question is what determines the orientation that an enzyme adopts when it is immobilised, and we have used Martini simulations to identify the mechanistic determinants of oriented enzyme immobilisation. We have also developed MartiniSurf, a tool for automated simulations of surface-immobilised biomolecular systems with Martini.
