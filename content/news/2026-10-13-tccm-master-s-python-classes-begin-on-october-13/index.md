## TCCM Master's Python Classes Begin on October 13

The **Python classes of the TCCM Master's programme** will begin on **October 13**. The classes will be taught by **David de Sancho** and will be open to **all members of the Kimika Teorikoa research group** interested in attending.

The course will cover the fundamentals of **Python programming and its use in scientific computing**, with practical examples and applications relevant to computational and theoretical chemistry.

The complete course materials are freely available in the [TCCM Python Notes](https://kimikateorikoa.github.io/python-notes-tccm/intro.html).

**All members of the group are welcome to join!**
