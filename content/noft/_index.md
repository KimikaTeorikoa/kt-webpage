Natural Orbital Functional Theory is one of the long-standing methodological pillars of the Donostia Kimika Teorikoa Laboratory. The NOFT line develops reduced-density-matrix-based electronic structure methods that aim to describe electron correlation with a cost that scales more favorably than wavefunction approaches while keeping a rigorous N-representability framework.

The subgroup is led by **Mario Piris** and combines formal developments, algorithm design and software implementation through the **DoNOF** program. Current activity spans PNOF and GNOF developments, efficient large-scale implementations, strongly correlated systems, excited states and connections with modern quantum and data-driven strategies.
