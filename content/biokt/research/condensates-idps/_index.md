Intrinsically disordered proteins are a central focus of our research. We use molecular simulations to understand how these proteins interact, both in coupled folding and binding to folded partners and with each other in biomolecular condensates. We have studied the competitive binding of HIF-1α and CITED2 to the TAZ1 domain of CBP, found a crossover in interaction strength between the aromatic amino acids tyrosine and phenylalanine in condensates, and identified the molecular determinants that distinguish arginine from lysine in cation–π interactions.
