We describe conformational transitions with master equation models, also known as Markov state models, which represent molecular kinetics as transitions between metastable states identified from simulations. We have applied these methods to protein and peptide folding and to gas diffusion within enzymes, and we develop new ways of building them, such as hierarchical density-based state assignment and coupling to reaction–diffusion simulations. Our open-source package [MasterMSM](https://github.com/daviddesancho/MasterMSM) makes the construction and analysis of these models available to others.
