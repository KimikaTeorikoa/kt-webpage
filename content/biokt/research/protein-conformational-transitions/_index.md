A major interest of the group is the simulation of protein conformational transitions such as folding and binding. We study ultrafast and downhill folding proteins, the coupled folding and binding of intrinsically disordered proteins, and the response of proteins to mechanical force, as probed in single-molecule force spectroscopy with optical tweezers or atomic force microscopes. We also develop analysis tools that resolve the molecular interactions driving these transitions along simulation trajectories.
