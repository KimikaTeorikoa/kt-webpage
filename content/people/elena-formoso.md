## Main Research Interest

- Investigation of aluminum in biological systems
- Anticancer drug design
- Study of conformational changes of proteins
- Study of enzymatic reactions
- Study of nanoclusters

## Scientific Career

- `02/01/2017-11/09/2018`: Postdoctoral fellow in the Theoretical Chemistry Group, Donostia, Basque Country.
- `02/01/2014-01/01/2017`: Postdoctoral fellow in the Theoretical Chemistry Group at the Donostia International Physics Center, Donostia, Basque Country.
- `02/01/2011-01/31/2014`: Postdoctoral fellow at the Universita della Svizzera Italiana and ETH Zurich, Lugano, Switzerland.
- `07/01/2010-01/31/2011`: Postdoctoral fellow in the Theoretical Chemistry Group at the University of the Basque Country, Donostia, Basque Country.
- `06/25/2010`: PhD in the Theoretical Chemistry Group at the University of the Basque Country, Donostia, Basque Country.

Advisor: Xabier Lopez

- `2009-06/30/2010`: Researcher position in the Theoretical Chemistry Group at the University of the Basque Country, Donostia, Basque Country.
